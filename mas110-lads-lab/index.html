Concept primer

Read these before opening the notebooks.

Pivot and target

pivot → target

The pivot is the nonzero anchor; the target is the entry in its column that we want to turn into zero.

Watch for: The teal source stays fixed while the amber target row changes.

Elimination factor

m = target / pivot

Choose the multiplier that makes target − factor × pivot equal exactly zero.

Watch for: The two contributions cancel in the highlighted column.

Row equivalence

Rᵢ ← Rᵢ − mRⱼ

Subtracting a multiple of one row from another rewrites the equations without changing their solutions.

Watch for: Only the target row changes; the solution set does not.
